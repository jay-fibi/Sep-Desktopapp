Our story

A neighborhood bookshop with a big heart

Founded in 1987, The Dusty Shelf began as a single shelf of second-hand paperbacks in the corner of a coffee shop. Today we stock over 35,000 titles across three floors — but we still believe every recommendation should come from a real reader, not an algorithm.

  • Weekly staff-pick shelves curated by our booksellers
  • Book clubs, author signings, and story time every Saturday
  • Free gift wrapping and local delivery on orders over $25
